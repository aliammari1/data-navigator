How should the app surface its ~28 apps and ~33 feature screens?

You have the capability already. The question is the shell that merchandises it. Three directions — pick one to anchor on, or mix.

A · Analytics OS (desktop-first)

Lean into the Puter metaphor. The windowed desktop becomes the default. Live widgets (today's KPIs, channel pulse, AI briefing) on the canvas, a richer category-organized Launchpad, and far more apps reachable from the dock. Features = apps you discover on a desktop.

B · Mission-Control hub (showcase home)

No windowing needed by default. A single curated landing: hero with today's KPIs + AI briefing, then capability sections (Analyze · Forecast · Report · Monitor · Collaborate) as live preview cards that launch each feature. Sidebar finally lists every feature. Polished SaaS-dashboard feel.

C · Command center (hybrid)

Keep the desktop's power, but make the home itself a showcase: a persistent category rail + top command bar (Ctrl+K → Commander), a merchandising home, and the ability to pop any feature into a floating window when you want OS-style multitasking. Best of both — more to build.

Click the direction that resonates (or tell me "mix A's widgets with B's home", etc.). I'll go deeper on whichever we anchor on.
